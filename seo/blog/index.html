Miller Pay login: the official way to sign in (2026 guide)

To log in to Miller Pay, open millerpay-official.website in any browser, tap Login, then enter the phone number you registered with and your password. That is the only official MillerPay login page — bookmark it so you never land on a lookalike domain.

Miller Pay app / APK download — how to install it safely

Miller Pay is a progressive web app, so there is no APK file to sideload. Open millerpay-official.website in Chrome, tap the browser menu and choose 'Add to Home screen'. You get an app icon that opens full-screen exactly like an installed Miller Pay app, with automatic updates and no storage cost.

Avoid any third-party 'Miller Pay APK download' site. Modified APKs can steal your login and wallet details. If it did not come from millerpay-official.website, it is not our app.

The installed app gives you the same home dashboard, USDT deposit (TRC20 / BEP20), INR quota orders, team invite code and withdrawal screens shown on our home page.

Miller Pay USDT to INR rate in India: ₹112 to ₹116

Most Indian P2P desks quote a single USDT price. Miller Pay uses three payout tiers: ₹116 per USDT to a premium UPI wallet (Paytm, PhonePe, Mobikwik), ₹114 for IMPS bank transfer and ₹112 for standard NEFT bank settlement.

The rate is locked the moment you confirm the order, so market movement during settlement does not change your payout. Deposits arrive on TRON TRC20 or BNB Chain BEP20; you pay only the network fee.

Example: selling 1,000 USDT on the premium tier pays ₹1,16,000, usually credited within minutes.

How the 4% + 2% = 6% INR order commission works

Every completed INR order on Miller Pay pays a 4% base commission plus a 2% settlement bonus — a flat 6%, credited to your quota balance instantly.

You buy a quota pack (for example 500 INR), the system matches incoming UPI orders to you, you confirm receipt, and the commission is added. There is no minimum volume and no hidden deduction.

Team commission is separate: share your invite code from the Team tab and earn on your downline's recharges as well.
