INR and UPI guide

Miller Pay INR orders: UPI workflow and payment-proof checklist

Follow the details displayed for the current order, retain payment evidence and confirm only after the recipient, amount and transaction status match.

By Miller Pay · Updated 12 October 2026 · India

Before accepting an INR order

  • Read the amount, UPI ID, account-holder name and time limit on the current order.
  • Use only the payment destination displayed inside the authenticated app.
  • Review the commission or bonus displayed for that order; do not assume an old rate.
  • Do not continue if someone asks for an extra payment outside the order.

Payment-proof workflow

  1. Complete the payment through the chosen UPI or banking app.
  2. Confirm that the banking app shows a successful transaction, not pending or failed.
  3. Save a screenshot that clearly shows amount, date and transaction reference.
  4. Upload the proof from the gallery in the order flow and submit it once.
  5. Keep the UTR or reference number until the order is finally completed.

If an order is delayed

Do not make a duplicate payment. Keep the screenshot and UTR, check the order status and contact only the support route shown on this official domain. A bank reversal and an app status update can occur at different times.
