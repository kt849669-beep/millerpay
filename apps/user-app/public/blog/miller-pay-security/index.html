Domain and fraud protection

Miller Pay security: verify the official domain before login

A familiar logo is not proof that a page is genuine. Verify the complete domain and protect your password, MPIN, OTP and wallet recovery phrase.

By Miller Pay · Reviewed 4 September 2026 · India

Official URLs

  • Brand and service information: millerpay-app.online/miller-pay/
  • Account access: millerpay-app.online/login

No unrelated registration or download domain is presented as official on this website. Bookmark the login page instead of searching for it each time.

Common warning signs

  • A spelling variation, added hyphen or different domain ending.
  • A request to install an APK sent through chat or a shortened URL.
  • A request for password, MPIN, OTP, private key or seed phrase.
  • Pressure to make another payment to unlock an existing transaction.
  • A support profile that cannot be reached from the official website.

If you suspect fraud

Stop entering data, take screenshots of the URL and messages, contact your bank quickly if money moved, and report suspected cyber or financial fraud through India’s National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. Do not confront the sender or send another payment.
