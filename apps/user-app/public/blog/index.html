USDT to INR rate in India: how Miller Pay pays ₹112 to ₹116

Most Indian P2P desks quote a single USDT price. Miller Pay uses three payout tiers: ₹116 per USDT to a premium UPI wallet (Paytm, PhonePe, Mobikwik), ₹114 for IMPS bank transfer and ₹112 for standard NEFT bank settlement.

The rate is locked the moment you confirm the order, so market movement during settlement does not change your payout. Deposits arrive on TRON TRC20 or BNB Chain BEP20; you pay only the network fee.

Example: selling 1,000 USDT on the premium tier pays ₹1,16,000, usually credited within minutes.

How the 4% + 2% = 6% INR order commission works

Every completed INR order on Miller Pay pays a 4% base commission plus a 2% settlement bonus — a flat 6%, credited to your quota balance instantly.

You buy a quota pack (for example 500 INR), the system matches incoming UPI orders to you, you confirm receipt, and the commission is added. There is no minimum volume and no hidden deduction.

Team commission is separate: share your invite code from the Team tab and earn on your downline's recharges as well.
